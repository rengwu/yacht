## Question 22

```text
Round: Round 3 — final behavior choices before mockups
Question: Beyond showing usage, what account actions belong in the dropdown?
Description (Markdown source):
A monitoring utility, a shortcut to a provider's site, and a tool that switches active CLI/app accounts have different scopes. Opening a provider site does not guarantee it opens the matching signed-in account. Actual switching would require further behavior and feasibility decisions.
Recommendation: Keep this version focused on monitoring, with refresh and settings. If account switching is essential to your workflow, choose it explicitly so the next session does not design a passive tracker by mistake.
Options: Monitoring only, with refresh and settings / Also let me open the provider's site or app / Also let me switch the active account used by the provider's CLI or app
Selected: Monitoring only, with refresh and settings
Notes: keep things simple please. no invented features. rule-lean
```
